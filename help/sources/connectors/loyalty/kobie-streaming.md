# [!DNL Kobie Streaming Events]

[!DNL Kobie Alchemy Loyalty Cloud (KALC)] is a configurable, secure, and scalable loyalty platform. Use the [!DNL Kobie Streaming Events] source to stream customer profiles, loyalty data, and transactional events from [!DNL Kobie] into Adobe Experience Platform.

By integrating [!DNL Kobie] with Experience Platform, you can:

* Stream loyalty points, tiers, and rewards in real time.
* Send transactional data into Experience Platform for analytics and activation.
* Use Real-Time Customer Profile to unify [!DNL Kobie] loyalty data with other Adobe profiles.

## Prerequisites

Before you connect [!DNL Kobie] to Adobe Experience Platform, ensure that you have the following:

* A valid Adobe Organization ID and access to an enabled Experience Platform sandbox.
* You must have both **[!UICONTROL View Sources]** and **[!UICONTROL Manage Sources]** permissions enabled for your account in order to connect your [!DNL Kobie] account to Experience Platform. Contact your product administrator to obtain the necessary permissions. For more information, read the [access control UI guide](../../../access-control/ui/overview.md).
* For prerequisite setup and mapping, contact your [!DNL Kobie Client Services] representative directly.

## Connect [!DNL Kobie Streaming Events] to Experience Platform

Once you have completed the prerequisite setup for [!DNL Kobie], read the [[!DNL Kobie Streaming Events] UI tutorial](../../tutorials/ui/create/loyalty/kobie-streaming.md) to learn how you can connect your account and stream data from [!DNL Kobie] to Experience Platform.
